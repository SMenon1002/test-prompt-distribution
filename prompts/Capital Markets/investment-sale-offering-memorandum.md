# Prompt Template: Investment Sale Offering Memorandum (OM) Summary

**Business Unit:** Capital Markets — Investment Properties

## Purpose
Generate a draft executive summary section for an Offering Memorandum on a property being brought to market.

## Fields to fill out before use
- **Property Name:** [e.g., 123 Main Street Office Tower]
- **Property Address / Market:** [City, State, Submarket]
- **Property Type:** [Office / Industrial / Multifamily / Retail / Mixed-Use]
- **Total SF / Unit Count:** [e.g., 250,000 SF or 180 units]
- **Year Built / Renovated:** [Year(s)]
- **Occupancy Rate:** [%]
- **In-Place NOI:** [$ amount]
- **Pro Forma / Stabilized NOI:** [$ amount]
- **Asking Price / Guidance:** [$ amount or "unpriced"]
- **Cap Rate (In-Place / Stabilized):** [%]
- **Major Tenants / Anchor Tenants:** [List names, lease expirations, WALT]
- **Sponsor / Seller Name:** [Entity name]
- **Key Investment Highlights (3-5 bullet points):** [e.g., irreplaceable location, below-market rents, value-add upside]
- **Broker Team & Contact Info:** [Names, titles, emails, phone numbers]
- **Submission / Call for Offers Date:** [Date]

## Prompt

You are a Capital Markets analyst at CBRE drafting the executive summary for an Offering Memorandum. Using the details below, write a compelling, professional 3-4 paragraph investment summary suitable for institutional and private capital investors. Emphasize the investment thesis, location fundamentals, and value creation opportunity. Maintain CBRE's professional tone — confident, data-driven, and free of hyperbole.

Property Name: {Property Name}
Address / Market: {Property Address / Market}
Property Type: {Property Type}
Size: {Total SF / Unit Count}
Year Built/Renovated: {Year Built / Renovated}
Occupancy: {Occupancy Rate}
In-Place NOI: {In-Place NOI}
Pro Forma NOI: {Pro Forma / Stabilized NOI}
Asking Price: {Asking Price / Guidance}
Cap Rate: {Cap Rate (In-Place / Stabilized)}
Major Tenants: {Major Tenants / Anchor Tenants}
Seller: {Sponsor / Seller Name}
Investment Highlights: {Key Investment Highlights}
Broker Contacts: {Broker Team & Contact Info}
Call for Offers Date: {Submission / Call for Offers Date}

Output the summary formatted for insertion into a PowerPoint/PDF OM, followed by a bulleted "Investment Highlights" section.
